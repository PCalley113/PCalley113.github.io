A small flame can turn into a major fatal fire in less than 30 seconds to 3 minutes
Inhaling the thick black smoke and posionous gases like cyanide causes most fire deaths.

Nearly two thirds of house fire deaths occur in homes with no smoke alarms or malfunctioning units.
Due to the thick black smoke that causes total darkness, you would be unable to see or locate the exit.
